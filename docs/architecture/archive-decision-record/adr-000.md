# ***ADR-000: Upgrade AlphaResearchOS Foundation from AI-Assisted Quant System to AI-Native Research Organization Framework***


## `Status:`

**Accepted**


## `Date:`

**2026-08-25**


## `Context:`

> AlphaResearchOS originally focused on building an AI-Native Fundamental Quant Alpha Research Operating System.

`The original objective:`

- systematic fundamental research;
- evidence-driven alpha discovery;
- quantitative validation;
- portfolio construction.


`During architecture review, it became clear that the project's deeper objective is not only alpha discovery.`

`The project is also exploring:`

`How professional knowledge work can be reorganized in the AI era through collaboration between:`

- human researchers;
- AI capabilities;
- deterministic software systems.


**✅Therefore the Foundation Document requires a conceptual upgrade.**


---

# ***🔶Decision:***

## `1. Upgrade project positioning:`

`AlphaResearchOS is defined as:`


> An AI-Native Research Organization framework,
with Quant Research as the first vertical application.


- The project contains three products:

```text
🔵Product A

AlphaResearchOS:

A system for AI-native quantitative research.


🔵Product B

Research Engineer Capability System:

A person capable of combining:

- Quant Research;
- Systems Engineering;
- AI Workflow Architecture;
- Domain Knowledge.


🔵Product C

AI-Native Research Organization methodology.
```

---

# `2. Establish Human-AI-System Boundary:`

```text
🔵Decision:

AI:

Responsible for:

- exploration;
- semantic understanding;
- evidence extraction;
- hypothesis generation.


🔵Deterministic Systems:

Responsible for:

- validation;
- statistics;
- risk;
- portfolio;
- execution.


🔵Human:

Responsible for:

- research objectives;
- economic interpretation;
- final judgment.
```

---

# `3. Introduce Enterprise Representation Layer`

`Decision:`

> Future Fundamental Quant should evolve from:

- Factor Engineering

`toward:`

> Enterprise Intelligence Representation.

>> System should model:

- financial reality;
- business context;
- industry dynamics;
- market perception;
- macro environment;
- alternative signals.


---

# `4. Introduce Research Organization Loop`


`Decision:`

- Alpha discovery is defined as a continuous learning loop:

```text
🔵Reality

   ↓

🔵Evidence

   ↓

🔵Representation

   ↓

🔵Hypothesis

   ↓

🔵Experiment

   ↓

🔵Falsification

   ↓

🔵Validation

   ↓

🔵Knowledge Memory

   ↓

🔵Portfolio
```

---

# `5. Preserve Core Principles:`


> The following principles remain unchanged:

- Evidence-first Quant;
- Point-in-time first;
- Reproducibility first;
- Deterministic capital gates;
- Human responsibility;
- Survival before optimization.


---

# `6. Consequences:`


## `Positive:`

> The project gains:

- clearer long-term positioning;
- stronger AI architecture identity;
- broader career applicability;
- stronger differentiation from traditional quant projects.


## `Trade-offs:`

- The project becomes broader.

> Therefore implementation must remain disciplined:

- Vision expands.

- Execution remains incremental.

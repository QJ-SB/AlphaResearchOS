# ***ADR-001: Harden AlphaResearchOS Research Governance and Capability Architecture***


## `Status:`

**Accepted**


## `Date:`

**2026-08-31**


## `Context:`

> Foundation v0.2 established the AI-Native Research Organization direction, but several research, capital, and career concepts still required clearer boundaries.

`The main issues identified:`

- Alpha / Beta / Benchmark / Risk Model could be misinterpreted across layers;
- research validation and capital deployment gates were not explicit enough;
- capacity, net alpha, attribution, and survival needed stronger governance;
- career strategy mixed long-term capability goals with short-term employment constraints;
- QD Engineering continuity was underrepresented in the yearly roadmap.


---

# ***🔶Decision:***

## `1. Harden Quant Research Semantics`

```text
🔵Decision:

Benchmark ≠ Risk Model

Active Return ≠ Alpha

Residual ≠ Alpha

Research Neutralization ≠ Portfolio Neutrality
```

> Alpha claims must be benchmark-relative, risk-adjusted, incremental, out-of-sample, cost-aware, and capacity-aware.


---

# `2. Strengthen Research and Capital Governance`

`Decision:`

> Upgrade the Alpha lifecycle to require:

```text
Candidate Feature
→ Risk / Benchmark Definition
→ Neutralization
→ Incrementality
→ Falsification
→ OOS
→ Net Alpha
→ Capacity
→ Portfolio
→ Attribution
→ Learning
```

- Capital deployment remains behind deterministic gates.
- Survival remains prior to optimization.


---

# `3. Adopt Mature-Frontier-First and Local-Validation-First`

`Decision:`

> Mature Quant / Finance methods should be learned, reproduced, and understood before local modification.

```text
Global Mature Method
→ Verify Preconditions
→ Localize
→ Validate with Local Evidence
```

> China-specific conclusions remain empirical hypotheses until locally validated.


---

# `4. Preserve One Core System with Two Capability Tracks`

`Decision:`

```text
AlphaResearchOS
   |
   ├─ Research Track
   |
   └─ QD Engineering Track
```

> Both tracks develop inside one core system and converge in the Portfolio + C++ Execution Bridge.

- Research remains the long-term capability expansion.
- QD Engineering remains a continuous near-term production capability requirement.
- AI-Native Engineering acts as a leverage layer across both.


---

# `5. Separate Permanent Architecture from Time-Bounded Career Strategy`

`Decision:`

> Career evidence and recruitment observations must not become permanent system principles.

```text
Permanent Career Principle
≠
Current Career Hypothesis
≠
2026–2027 Roadmap
```

> Current near-term positioning:

```text
QD-first
+
Research-literate
+
AI-differentiated
```

is treated as a time-bounded roadmap decision, not a permanent identity.


---

# `6. Consequences`

## `Positive:`

- clearer Alpha / Beta / Benchmark semantics;
- stronger research-to-capital governance;
- better separation of research validity from portfolio decisions;
- capacity and attribution become first-class concerns;
- roadmap better aligns research growth with real production engineering requirements.


## `Trade-offs:`

- research artifacts require more explicit metadata and validation;
- fewer ideas can reach capital quickly;
- roadmap execution becomes more disciplined and evidence-dependent.


---

# `7. Result`

> These decisions are incorporated into:

```text
AlphaResearchOS Foundation v0.3 Final Freeze
```

> Foundation v0.3 becomes the current architecture baseline; v0.2 remains the historical frozen baseline.

# Research State Directory Instruction

## Responsibility

`research-state.md` is the rolling summary of active research questions, hypotheses, evidence status, falsifiers, conclusions permitted by current evidence, and research risks.

## Update Contract

- Use the standard rolling-state header and increment the revision only for canonical state change.
- Separate hypotheses from established findings and permitted claims.
- Name the information set, benchmark, risk model, evidence, and falsifier when applicable.
- Keep detailed reproducible runs in `../../research-log/experiment-registry.md`.
- Preserve meaningful negative results in the registry and summarize only current implications here.
- Never call Active Return or a historical Residual Alpha without benchmark-relative, risk-adjusted, incremental, OOS, cost, and capacity evidence.
- Use explicit unknown markers; do not invent a hypothesis, experiment, dataset, result, or conclusion.

## Authority

Reproducible evidence and the Experiment Registry outrank this narrative summary. A checkpoint or Sidecar Return can propose a change only after freshness, evidence, and human review.
